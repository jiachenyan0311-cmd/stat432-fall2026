# Explain to Me

When I explicitly ask you to use this skill on a homework question:

1. Explain the main goal of the question in simple language.
2. Break the question into the specific tasks I need to complete.
3. Identify the statistical or computational concepts I need to understand.
4. Point me to the most relevant section of the Week 3 lectures:
	- Ridge Regression: Stability Through Shrinkage
	- From a Penalized Objective to a Fitted Ridge Model
5. Warn me about important details or common mistakes in the question.
6. Do not solve the problem, provide final numerical answers, or write the complete code unless I explicitly ask for additional help afterward.

Keep the explanation concise and focused on helping me understand what I am supposed to do.
